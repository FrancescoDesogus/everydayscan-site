EverydayScan

EverydayScan is a personal Android app by Francesco Desogus that scans documents and ID cards into PDFs, lists them with folders and a search that reads the text inside them, and copies text seen through the camera.

It is made for one person and isn't published on any store. The PDFs stay on the phone and, when Google Drive is connected, in a folder of the user's own Drive.
